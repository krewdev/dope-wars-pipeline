# PLAY — DOPE WARS: THE PIPELINE

Satirical 30-day trading terminal. Recipes are item IDs plus a purity roll. No chemistry. Beacons are fiction. GPS hashes to a cell; raw coordinates are not stored.

Open `DOPE_WARS_PIPELINE.html` on your phone, or drop it on a GitHub Pages repo as `index.html` (HTTPS is required for SHARE FIX).

The chrome at https://krewdev.github.io/ is a stub until that file overwrites `index.html`.

## The plot is the tutorial

There is no hint list. The HUD **JOB** line is the only instruction. Dawn repeats a missed verb twice, in character, then the season moves on.

| Day | Voice | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 THE COAT | Radio | FARM GREEN |
| 2 THE TICKET | Harry | JET QUEENS |
| 3 THE FLAG | Radio | Open MAP |
| 4 THE LIST | Kit Row | JOIN a crew |
| 5 THE STALL | Kit Row | BUY CATALYST |
| 6 THE ROLL | Radio | COOK GREEN + CATALYST |
| 7 THE METER | Harry | PAY HARRY |

After those marks, the story is the season: shoes, flags, sell, vest, docks.

## HUD

DAY / CASH / DEBT / CITY / HEAT / ACTS / CREW / COAT / CELL / JOB

Three acts per day. WAIT or END DAY advances the calendar. Idle days decay heat. Harry adds 10% at dawn.

## Verbs

| Verb | What it does |
|---|---|
| FARM | Buy 10 plants. GREEN is cheapest in Brooklyn. |
| KIT | Buy one kit. Stall markup depends who holds the corner. |
| COOK | Burn recipe IDs + $50. Purity ≥80 CERT (vest 3 days). 55–79 STREET. below CUT. |
| SELL | Dump CUT / STREET / CERT. Holder takes 8% on a corner. CERT locked until vest lifts. |
| JET | $80 ticket, 1 act, change city. |
| MAP | 17 fictional beacons. Flags and counts only. No player pins. |
| SPIN | Shoes on the wire. FIX: 5 min cooldown. RADIO: 1 act. Third spin same shoe same day = DUST. |
| JOIN | Pick a crew. Specialty stalls go cheap if your crew holds the corner. |
| SHARE FIX | Opt-in GPS. Hash only. Walk into a new cell = free snap, 0 day. |
| RADIO | Deny GPS. Season still runs. Shadow borough. |
| RAID | Contest a market. Win plants your flag. Fail adds heat. |
| BUY OFF | 3× last drip. Flag without a fight. |
| WIPE | Burn 1 CERT or 1 SHOT. Node UNHELD until tomorrow. |
| PAY | Send cash to Harry. |
| WAIT | End the day. Drip pays on corners you hold. |

## Cities

Brooklyn · Queens · Manhattan · The Bronx · Coney Island · Bridgeport

Start: $2200 cash, $5500 debt, Brooklyn, Greenpoint Radio, 1 SHOT, coat 100.

## THE BLOCK

Eight crews. Seventeen preselected beacons (wires + markets). See `THE_BLOCK.md`.

Cartel = one crew holding ≥3 markets.

Density drip at dawn: RURAL $8 / STRIP $22 / METRO $55 / LANDMARK $90, times `(1 + 0.08 × teammates)`, cap 5.

## Clocks

- 30-day season
- Harry 10% each dawn
- 3 acts per day
- CERT vest 3 days
- Heat decays only when you idle

## Endings (Day 30)

Evidence Locker · Off the Board · Cartel Season · Left Standing · Flat · Harry Kept the Saw

## Safety

Game only. A wipe closes a claim, it is not a method. Chat strips coordinates and “meet me at.”
